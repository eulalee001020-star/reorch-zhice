`p0_ulster_mes` does not contain the unavailable official MES source. Its
surrogate samples, digital-twin fixtures and customer-admission rehearsal data
are generated engineering fixtures. The pack deliberately remains
`acquisition_blocked` until source provenance and customer authorization are
available.

These datasets and derived packs support reproducible engineering evaluation.
They do not establish customer production reliability, planner adoption,
financial ROI or production writeback authorization.
